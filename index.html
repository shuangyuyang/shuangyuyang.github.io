About

I joined Jinan University (Guangzhou, China) as an Assistant Professor at the Institute for Economic and Social Research in August 2023. I received my Ph.D. in economics from the National University of Singapore. My research interests are decision-making under uncertainty and bounded rationality, and more generally behavioral and experimental economics.
